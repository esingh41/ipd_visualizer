    Curves

    Ready.

    IPD curves are this app’s induced-dipole induction energies, one per damping scheme. Universal applies a single Thole parameter to every atom; the radius variants scale damping by TS / MBIS atomic radii, and differ in whether damping is applied within a monomer (intra), between monomers (inter), or both. The Reference curves are the induction energies those models are trying to reproduce, grouped by level of theory — a dataframe carrying several gets several groups. Each level’s total is drawn solid. Where the source also decomposes it, the components are dashed and sum to the total exactly; SAPT2+ here supplies only the total, which is a complete reference rather than a partial one.
    Coordinates in Ångström, induced dipoles in atomic units (e·bohr). Frame 0 is the SCF’s direct-field seed, not zero.
