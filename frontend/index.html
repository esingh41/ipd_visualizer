    Ready.

    IPD curves are this app’s induced-dipole induction energies, one per damping scheme. Universal applies a single Thole parameter to every atom; the radius variants scale damping by TS / MBIS atomic radii, and differ in whether damping is applied within a monomer (intra), between monomers (inter), or both. The SAPT reference curves are the SAPT0 induction decomposition those models are trying to reproduce — drawn solid for the total and dashed for its three components, which sum to it exactly.
    Coordinates in Ångström, induced dipoles in atomic units (e·bohr). Frame 0 is the SCF’s direct-field seed, not zero.
